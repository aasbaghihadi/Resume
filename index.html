HOW I THINK

Create a better value–cost boundary.

One of the questions I enjoy most is simple:

Can we make the customer experience meaningfully better while also making the business economics better?

Sometimes the answer is a new feature. Sometimes it is a simpler offer, a different packaging logic, a new partner model, a different route to market—or a different way of defining the problem.

The goal is not “more value at any cost.”
It is value that the business can sustain.
